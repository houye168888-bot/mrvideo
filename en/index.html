ABOUT US

About Us

Mr.Video is a full-service new media agency rooted in Myanmar, operated by a senior team from China. We cover corporate promotion, social media management, talent training, short-video e-commerce, ad placement and influencer incubation — providing end-to-end support from strategy, shooting and editing through to media buying and talent development. Guided by our philosophy of "only creating traffic that brings value", we pair proven traffic playbooks with the realities of the Myanmar market to empower local businesses and brands, turning traffic into visible business growth and lasting brand equity.
